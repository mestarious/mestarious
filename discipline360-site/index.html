The rule is brutal on purpose

A day is a WIN only if you do all three.

Miss even one and the day is a loss. No half-credit. No excuses. The app doesn't care about your intentions — only what you actually did.

  1. 01

    Beat the alarm

    A snooze-proof mission — math, typing, shake or tap — stands between you and the off button. No cheating your way back to sleep.

  2. 02

  3. 03

    One honest entry. A cozy journal with 20 handwriting fonts and animated backgrounds — the daily reckoning that closes the loop.

Everything is built to be un-cheatable

Snooze-proof missions

Math, typing, shake and tap missions with low/mid/high difficulty. Engineered to survive aggressive OEM battery-killers so it rings on the exact second.

Native step counter

Reads the hardware step sensor as a foreground service — counts in the background, survives reboots, and never double-counts.

Deep focus timer

Titled, recorded focus sessions build a track record of real work — not vibes. Your effort, logged.

App blocker

Bilingual · EN + AR
