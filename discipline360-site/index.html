Before the excuses, the truth

It starts by confronting your real numbers.

The setup isn't a friendly tour — it's a diagnostic. Discipline 360 measures your screen time, your daily pickups, and the trigger behind them, then shows you exactly what they cost: the days you lose every year, the 23 minutes gone with every pickup.

Then you choose one life domain to transform in 90 days — physical, financial, mental, relationships or legacy — and the app builds you a personalized, hour-by-hour blueprint with a real target. You even name your winner self and your loser self, so the fight has a face.

  • BMI, income-gap and knowledge-gap engines
